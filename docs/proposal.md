## 1. The problem

Traveling requires a lot of planning. Destination, tickets, hotels, reservations, all these and more can be such a hassle. In fact, people may not even know where their getaway destination is. In my app, you can give as many information or as simple as a vibe and it will give you a selection of destinations, itineraries, and budget to fit your fit.

## 2. Who uses this

Anyone can use my app. Students can use it for breaks, weekend trips, or even study abroad adventures. Couples can us it to plan their romantic getaways or honeymoon. Solo travelers can find a personalized itinerary that fits their plan. Anyone who is interested in traveling can use this app.

**Primary user:**

Students, couples, schools, business travelers, adventure seekers, etc.

**What they do today instead:**

Quick search with google for a vague idea to base off their journey. It is messy, there is no budget and no solid plan. Coming up with a itinerary takes time and that might be what's holding people back from their greatest adventures. 

**Why they'd switch:**

1. AI will help you build your trip so you are not in this alone (aka blind leading the blind)
2. There will be clear a plan, budget, and you aren't going into this blind or overwhelmed
3. Vacations/trips should be relaxing, and that can start at the planning phase
   

## 3. Tools and services

Neon of database and Render for the web service. ChatGPT and API as the AI services. TailwindCSS and React for the frontend (aesthetic and display).
